Pounce On It

by Out of Office Travels

Award space, spotted. A Chrome extension that captures real award availability from your own logged-in airline sessions — no backend, no data feed, no subscription.

SIN  ✈  FRA  ·  42,500 MI  ·  SAVER

Your own airline session is the data source. Pounce On It records the award space those pages actually show you — non-phantom by definition, because it came from the airline's own logged-in site.

Capture

While you browse an airline's award search, the extension records the cabins, miles and dates it returns.

Scan

Add target routes, press Start, and one pass checks every route × programme × date. It stops itself when the pass is complete.

Review

A search-first board over everything captured: filter by stops, times, airlines, programme, cabin, tier, miles or duration — then export.

Local-first by design

There is no Pounce On It server. Captures, settings and scan progress live in your browser's local storage on your device. No analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, no third-party data sharing.

Supported programmes

  • United MileagePlus
  • Singapore Airlines KrisFlyer
  • Cathay Pacific Asia Miles
  • Air Canada Aeroplan
  • British Airways Club (Avios)
  • Air France/KLM Flying Blue

Privacy

Read the full privacy policy. The short version: the extension collects nothing, transmits nothing and sells nothing. Everything it records stays on your device.
